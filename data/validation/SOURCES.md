# Validation data sources

## `TUB_M50LT_*.csv`
Derived from: Jiaqi Yao, Droese, Kowal, **"Lithium-Ion Battery Internal Temperature Dataset"**, Technische
Universität Berlin, Department of Electrical Energy Storage Technology (EET), 2026.
DOI [10.14279/depositonce-25110](https://doi.org/10.14279/depositonce-25110) — licensed
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Cells: LG INR21700 M50LT (NMC, 4.93 Ah nominal), drilled and fitted with an internal Type K thermocouple, resealed
with epoxy; single cells in a climate chamber; 12 drive cycles from 100 % SOC (after CC-CV charge and 2 h rest) to
2.5 V at 5/15/25/35/45 °C.

Changes made by us (`scripts/convert_tuberlin.py`, from the `2_preprocessed` folder): current sign flipped to
+ = discharge; columns renamed to `time_s, current_a, voltage_v, temp_c` (surface), `temp_core_c` (internal),
`soc` (reference, coulomb counting); values rounded. No other filtering beyond the publishers' preprocessing.

`*_CHG-<cycle>_*` logs are the 1C CC-CV charges (4.93 A to 4.2 V) that follow each drive cycle, cut from the
publishers' raw test files (`0_raw/*.parquet`) together with the 2 h rest after them, resampled to 1 s, current sign
flipped. Sensor channels were identified by matching the raw file to the preprocessed one: `Temperature2` = surface
(`temp_c`), `Temperature3` = internal (`temp_core_c`); `Temperature4` (another probe, missing on cell 105) is not
used. `soc` = charged Ah / total charged Ah (0 at the 2.5 V cut-off). `ambient_c` = surface reading at the end of the
rest, when cell and chamber are in equilibrium.

Fit on cell AM23NMC00103 at 25 and 35 °C (US06, HWFET, charge after US06); everything at 45 °C is hold-out. Record:
`calibration_nmc21700_5ah.json` (`scripts/calibrate_nmc.py`).

Used as a **proxy cell** for the generic "NMC 21700 5 Ah class" cell — not as the design cell itself.

## `SYNTHETIC_*.csv`
Generated by our own model plus sensor noise (`scripts/make_synthetic_log.py`). A method check for the fitter —
**not evidence**.
